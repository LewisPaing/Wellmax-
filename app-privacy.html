App Privacy Policy

Effective: August 22, 2026

This policy explains how WellMax Advertising & Media handles information when you use the WellMax iOS app and its connected client portal.

Information you provide

If you send a project enquiry, contact us by email, or use a client account, we may receive information such as your name, company, email address, project details, login information and files that you choose to provide.

App and portal usage

The native app does not use advertising trackers. The client portal and pages opened from wellmaxagency.com may use necessary cookies, security logs and aggregated analytics to operate the service, prevent abuse and understand usage.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested agency services.
  • To authenticate clients and deliver project information or files.
  • To maintain security, diagnose problems and improve the app and portal.
  • To meet legal, accounting or contractual obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate our website, email, analytics, hosting or client portal, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security protections. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. You can request access, correction, account deletion or deletion of submitted personal information by emailing us. Some records may be retained where legally required.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email wellmaxadvertisingmedia@gmail.com.
